Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a plain-language starting template, not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Review it with a qualified advocate before relying on it for paying clients.

1. What I collect

When you submit the contact form or message me on WhatsApp/email, I collect only what you provide: your name, business name, email, phone number, and the details of your enquiry.

2. How I use it

Information you submit is used solely to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the agreed service. I do not sell or rent your information to third parties, and I do not use it for marketing without your separate consent.

3. Data from footprint audits

Where you engage me for a Public Footprint Audit, any findings about your business's public online presence are shared only with you and treated as confidential under the Terms & NDA. This information is not stored beyond what is needed to deliver the report, and is deleted on request once the engagement ends.

4. Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019

I aim to handle personal data in line with the principles of Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019: collecting only what is necessary, obtaining consent, and keeping data secure. I am not a registered Data Protection Officer; for formal DPA compliance work, you should engage a licensed consultant.

5. Third-party tools

The contact form on this site is processed by a third-party form service (Formspree) and messages sent via WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. I do not control how these platforms handle data in transit.

6. Your rights

You may ask me at any time what information I hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion, by emailing mungaikaringa5@gmail.com.

7. Retention

Contact enquiries that do not become paid engagements are deleted after 12 months. Records for completed paid engagements are kept for basic bookkeeping purposes as required by law.
