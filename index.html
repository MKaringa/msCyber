Kenya Today

These are the attacks Kenyan businesses face daily

Sourced from the Communications Authority of Kenya (CA) Quarterly Sector Statistics Report and the National KE-CIRT/CC. Figures current as of August 2026.

Business Email Compromise & Phishing

Fake invoices from "suppliers" and calls from "the CEO" trick staff into transferring money. The CA reports that attackers increasingly use AI to write more convincing phishing messages and impersonate executives.

Outdated Websites & System Attacks

That 3.37 billion figure isn't abstract — most of it comes from unpatched software. Over 120 Kenyan business websites were defaced between May 2025 and March 2026, mostly through outdated website software and plugins.

Password & MFA Weaknesses

Reused passwords are tested against email, bank, and accounting logins. The CA recorded over 46 million brute-force attempts in Q1 2026 alone. An app-based authenticator stops these attempts cold, even when a password has already leaked.
